How to play

Stack the falling pieces and clear lines. A double-size piece comes every 10 or 20 turns.

Scoring

  • Clearing lines: 100 × 2(n−1) points for n lines cleared at once — 1 line = 100, 2 = 200, 3 = 400, 4 = 800, and it keeps doubling.
  • Drop / hard drop: 1 point for every row the piece falls.

採点
